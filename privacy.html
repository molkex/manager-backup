Privacy policy

Manager Backup is used only by its owner. It requests the drive.file scope, which gives access only to files the app itself creates in the owner's Google Drive.

  • It uploads encrypted backup files to a single folder and reads nothing else in Google Drive.
  • No data is collected from, shared with, or sold to anyone.
  • No analytics, advertising, or third-party processing.
